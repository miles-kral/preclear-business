About PreClear

Building a safer decision point before trust.

PreClear is building proactive cybersecurity infrastructure designed to help organizations evaluate external files before those files reach the people, systems, and environments they are meant to protect.

Why PreClear exists

Too many security decisions happen after exposure.

Every day, organizations receive files from customers, applicants, vendors, partners, contractors, and other outside sources. Those files often move quickly into internal workflows where employees are expected to decide whether they can be trusted.

Traditional security controls remain essential, but much of cybersecurity is still designed around detecting or responding to threats after they have already entered an environment.

PreClear is built around a different question: what if organizations could make a meaningful trust decision before access occurs?

What we're building

Pre-ingress security infrastructure.

01

Inspect

Evaluate external files before they enter normal internal workflows.

02

Decide

Turn technical evidence into a clear, understandable trust decision.

03

Record

Preserve the decision and its supporting evidence as part of an auditable history.

04

Prove

Give organizations evidence they can use for reporting, governance, and accountability.

Our mission

Help organizations know before they trust.

Our mission is to make proactive trust decisions a practical part of everyday cybersecurity. We want organizations to have a clear way to evaluate unfamiliar content before exposing employees or internal systems to unnecessary risk.

PreClear is not intended to replace the security technologies organizations already depend on. It is designed to add an upstream decision layer that strengthens the controls already in place.

How we think about security

Proactive protection should also be understandable.

Prevention before reaction

Reduce unnecessary exposure by moving meaningful evaluation earlier in the workflow.

Evidence over guesswork

Give people and organizations understandable reasons behind every trust decision.

Accountability by design

Preserve decisions and supporting evidence so security activity can be reviewed later.

Security that works with people

Build controls that reduce uncertainty without forcing employees to become cybersecurity experts.

Built to grow

From small organizations to enterprise environments.

PreClear Business is designed to give smaller organizations practical protection while creating a foundation that can grow into more advanced reporting, governance, integrations, and organizational control.

Our longer-term goal is to establish pre-ingress security as a durable layer within modern cybersecurity architecture.
