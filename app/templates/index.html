Why PreClear Business exists

Most security protects systems. PreClear protects the decision before entry.

Every day, employees are asked to trust resumes, contracts, invoices, customer documents, downloads, and attachments from outside the organization.

PreClear creates a controlled moment before that trust is granted. It evaluates the file, communicates the recommendation, and preserves the evidence behind the decision.
