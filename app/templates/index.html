What PreClear checks

One trust decision for everything that arrives.

Files, links, emails, text messages and QR codes each get their own checks, and every result is recorded the same way in your Decision Ledger.

Files

Attachments, downloads and uploads are inspected for what they really are, not just what they're called.

  • Programs disguised as documents
  • Macro-enabled and password-locked files
  • Threat intelligence by file fingerprint

Links & Text Messages

Paste a link, or a whole text, Teams or Slack message. PreClear checks the wording and shows who really owns every link.

  • Lookalike domains imitating brands
  • Shortened and hidden destinations
  • Toll, delivery and gift-card text scams

Emails

Upload a saved email or paste its original to check the sender, what the message asks for, and everything inside it.

  • Spoofed senders and mismatched reply addresses
  • Changed bank details and gift card requests
  • Every link and attachment, checked together

QR Codes

Take a photo with your phone through PreClear, or upload an image or PDF. See where a code leads before you scan it.

  • Payment and parking-sign scams
  • Codes hidden in emails and PDF invoices
  • Wi-Fi, payment and account-setup codes explained
Links are never visited PreClear checks every address without opening it, so nothing on the other end knows it was examined.
Less data kept Text messages and email body text aren't stored, and Wi-Fi passwords or account keys in QR codes are removed before anything is recorded.
Evidence you can show Every check produces an Evidence Record with the reasons behind the decision, ready to export as a PDF.

PreClear provides decision support, not a guarantee that a file, link, email, text message or QR code is safe. When something asks you to act, confirm it another way.
